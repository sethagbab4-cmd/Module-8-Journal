When developing a program, it is important to understand what the user wants and what they are
trying to accomplish before I begin coding. User stories help this process because they give
a description of what feature the customer wants from their perspective. This helps me create 
a program that meets their requirements and expectations instead of going based on my own assumptions.
With development, I want to incorporate Agile processes such as testing continuously and receiving feedback
constantly throughout the process. These make identifying problems easier, and I am better able to adjust to
changes. To be a good team member in software development, I have to communicate effectively with my teammates.
I need to listen to others' ideas, make sure I am focused on my responsibilities, and be open to feedback. These
traits help the development process go more smoothly so the team can produce a good product.
